# OctoAcme Project Management Docs

This repository contains the core project management process documents for OctoAcme. The team follows a simple lifecycle for delivering work: initiation, planning, execution, release, and retrospective. These practices are designed to align stakeholders, reduce risk, and help the team deliver value through predictable, iterative execution.

## Project Management Process Summary

OctoAcme begins each initiative by validating the business need and defining success outcomes. During initiation, the team confirms the problem statement, identifies stakeholders, clarifies the goal, and creates a lightweight one-pager that captures the problem, targets, risks, and initial resource needs. Once stakeholders agree on the value and priority, the team moves into planning, breaking the work into a prioritized backlog with milestones, dependencies, and clear acceptance criteria.

During execution, the team manages day-to-day progress with daily standups, weekly syncs, and visible tracking on the project board. Work is broken into small, testable increments, each with clear ownership and quality gates. The process emphasizes transparent communication, risk escalation, and ongoing coordination across product, project, engineering, and QA roles so blockers are surfaced early and decisions are made with the right stakeholders.

Quality and release practices are built into the workflow rather than left to the end. The team uses automated checks, security scanning, smoke tests, and manual QA where needed to validate that features meet acceptance criteria before release. When work moves to production, OctoAcme standardizes deployment checks, communication plans, rollback triggers, and post-deploy verification so releases are safer and easier to manage under pressure.

At the end of each sprint, milestone, or release, the team closes the loop through retrospectives and continuous improvement. Action items are tracked, owners are assigned, and lessons learned are fed back into the backlog so teams improve their process over time. This ensures OctoAcme remains customer-focused, iterative, and transparent across the full project lifecycle.

## How These Docs Fit Together

- Initiation establishes the business case and direction.
- Planning turns the initiative into milestones, backlog items, and delivery expectations.
- Execution tracks progress, risks, dependencies, and team communication.
- Release and deployment standardize safe delivery to production.
- Retrospectives capture lessons and drive ongoing improvements.

These documents provide a practical framework for cross-functional project delivery and a shared reference point for the OctoAcme team.
